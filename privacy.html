Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Racebib app ("Racebib", "we") collects, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. The data controller is Buproject, contactable at info@buproject.net.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account: email address or Sign in with Apple identifier, your nameTo create your account, sign you in and show your name on your profile and posts
Training profile: sport, target race, division, 5 km / 10 km / swim / bike fitness, training days, equipment, goal timeTo build and adapt your training plan and pacing
Training and race logs: sessions you complete, split times, race results you time or addTo show progress and history and adjust your plan
Community content: posts, likes, meetups, partner invites, reports and blocksTo run the crew feed, partner search and meetups, and to keep the community safe
Coach conversationsTo answer your questions (see "AI coach" below)
Purchases: subscription status and an anonymous purchase identifierTo unlock Racebib Athlete and restore purchases

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or health data. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell personal data.

Who can see what

Your posts, name and city are visible to other signed-in Racebib users. In partner search you are only visible if you turn on "Show me to others racing", which shares your name, city, division and target time with athletes racing the same event. You can turn it off at any time.

AI coach

The coach is powered by Claude, provided by Anthropic PBC. When you message the coach, we send your messages and a short training summary (sport, race and date, plan week, today's session, target and predicted time, training days) to Anthropic to generate a reply. We do not send your name or email. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data is not used to train their models. The app asks for your agreement before your first message.

Service providers

  • Supabase (database, authentication, server functions) — data stored in the EU (Frankfurt)
  • Anthropic (AI coach replies)
  • RevenueCat and Apple / Google (subscriptions and payments; we never see your card details)
  • Open-Meteo (race-day weather; only the race location is sent)

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, training data, results, posts, likes, coach messages and invites are permanently deleted.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account in the app: Me → Delete account.
  • Access, correct or export your data, or object to processing, by emailing info@buproject.net. We reply within 30 days.
  • If you are in the EU/UK you can complain to your data protection authority.

Our legal bases are performing our contract with you (running the app), your consent (AI coach, partner visibility) and our legitimate interest in keeping the community safe.

Children

Racebib is not intended for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
