Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Racebib. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Or in the app: Me → Subscription → Change or cancel plan.

I bought Racebib Athlete on another device.
Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, then Me → Subscription → Restore purchases.

How do I delete my account?
Me → Delete account. This permanently deletes your data.

How do I report or block someone?
Tap the ••• on a post (or More on an athlete) and choose Report or Block. Unblock in Me → Blocked athletes.

A race date looks wrong.
Dates come from the organisers. Email us and we'll fix the calendar.
