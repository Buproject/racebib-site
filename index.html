Racebib: Hybrid & Tri Coach

An AI training plan, race-day pacing and race-trip planner for HYROX racers and triathletes (full and half distance).

  • A week-by-week plan built around your race date, your fitness and your equipment
  • An AI coach for pacing, sore spots, travel and swapped sessions
  • Pacing targets for every run, station and leg, and a race-day split timer
  • Race-trip planning: time difference, weather, race-week plan
  • A community crew with partner search and local meetups hosted by Racebib Ambassadors
