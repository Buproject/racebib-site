Racebib Ambassador guide

Thanks for leading your city. A few basics:

Hosting a meetup

  • Pick a public place or a gym that has agreed to host you.
  • Set a realistic number of spots and keep sessions open to all levels.
  • Brief everyone on the session and scale it for beginners.

Safety

  • Ask people to train within their limits and stop if something hurts.
  • Know where the nearest first-aid kit and exit are.
  • Never collect money through Racebib. Meetups are free community events.

Community

  • Welcome first-timers by name and introduce them to others.
  • Report anyone who breaks the community guidelines.
